Key Insights
→Students obsessively track attendance % to stay above the 75% eligibility cutoff
→Assignment deadlines are missed because reminders live across WhatsApp, notice boards, and email
→Timetable changes (room/time shifts) reach students late or not at all
→College announcements get buried under personal chat notifications
→Students want one login, not five separate portals/apps for academic info
→Dark mode usage: most students keep phone dark mode on permanently

HMW Statement

How might we help college students track their classes, attendance, and deadlines in one place — with zero missed notices?

Design Thinking Process

💡
1 — Empathise
22 student interviews across 3 colleges, plus review of existing college portals. Found students treat academic apps as anxiety sources (missed deadlines, attendance drop) rather than useful tools.
InterviewsPortal audit
🧩
2 — Define
Synthesised into three problem themes: Information scatter, Deadline blindness, and Notification overload from irrelevant channels.
Affinity mapHMW statement
💭
3 — Ideate
Brainstormed 40+ concepts, dot-voted to 5. Key bets: single dashboard for today's classes, live attendance %, and unified notifications.
BrainstormDot voting
✏️
4 — Prototype
Paper sketches → these lo-fi wireframes → next stage is an interactive Figma prototype. Prioritised 5 core screens covering the daily-use happy path.
Paper sketchLo-fi wireframe
🧪
5 — Test (planned)
5-student usability test targeting a sub-2-minute "check today's schedule" task. Task completion rate and SUS score as KPIs.
Usability testSUS score
🎯
Design Decisions
Dashboard leads with today's classes, not a generic home feed. Attendance % surfaced prominently, not buried in a menu. Assignments include an inline reminder CTA.
